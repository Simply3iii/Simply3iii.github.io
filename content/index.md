I'm in the first year of a PhD at the University of Edinburgh, on the Dependable and Deployable
AI for Robotics programme, supervised by Adam Stokes.

The short version of the project: a self-driving lab works out how to make something, then a
row of cheap little machines makes it. I'm working on the software and AI that runs them, and
on how that AI can tell when its own readings have gone wrong.

This year is mostly groundwork. I'm reading, taking the programme's courses, learning C++ for
the firmware side, and building my first bench rig: one pump, a camera and a set of scales.
I'll post here as it comes together.
