Self-driving labs are machines that plan an experiment, run it, measure the result and decide
what to try next. They're good at finding a recipe. Making something in quantity is a different
problem.

My PhD looks at the step after discovery: copying what one lab machine has found onto a fleet of
cheap, identical production cells, and running that fleet like a small data centre. The part I
care most about is dependability. A blocked channel doesn't fail loudly. It produces readings
that look fine and are wrong, so the system needs to notice that and not learn from them.

It's early days. I'll write about it in the [log](/log/) as I build things.
